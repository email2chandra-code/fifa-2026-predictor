How to use

  1. Head-to-head: type a few letters (e.g. Mex) under Team 1 & Team 2, pick from the list, then Predict.
  2. Groups & knockout: type to search or tap the list — every team box is editable.
  3. Group stage: each group has 4 teams — pick 1st, 2nd, and Best 3rd; the 4th team is shown on each card (updates as you type).
  4. Optional: Load model picks pre-fills groups from the model — you can still edit every name after.
  5. Optional: Show tournament odds opens the model’s QF / SF / Final / Win probabilities while you fill in groups.
  6. Your picks auto-save on this device.
  7. Schedule: venue time is stadium local; pick your timezone (or use auto) for Your time.
